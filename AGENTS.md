# Repository workflow

Whenever an agent or chat implements a material change in this repository, it must review and update `PROJECT_STATE.md` in the same task, after the implementation and verification are complete and before reporting completion. It must also update `docs/PROJECT_OVERVIEW_AND_SPLIT_PAYMENT_PLAN.md` when the change affects the documented features, readiness, limitations, or roadmap.

Do not mark planned, proposed, discussed, or documentation-only work as implemented. Plans must remain clearly labelled as plans until the corresponding application or deployment change has been completed and verified. Record the date, material implementation changes, current readiness, verification performed, and any new limitations or required next steps. Never place credentials, tokens, passwords, or other secrets in either document.
